# Saved Gaussian-product PPC: isolated 0.0.35.9000 R-only candidate

The current reader admits complete existing
`bmeas_iid_gaussian_product_context_v1` capsules for population and conditional
Gaussian PPC. No new producer profile, migration, reclassification or reseal is
introduced. Diagnostic refresh remains unavailable for this product profile.
Earlier no-PPC notices describe the historical producer, whose capability record
and protected evidence remain unchanged. They are not rewritten by this reader.

## Scientific scope

Only canonical Gaussian descriptors with iid common-plus-parameter-residual
coordinates and complete retained product records are admitted. Common-product,
residual-product with no common layer, fixed/MGS/one-label common layers, and dual
product records use the same existing Gaussian forward law. Ordered coordinates,
sample-centered product laws, mixed/non-Gaussian families and incomplete product
records are refused. This adds no generic callback or native execution route.

Each selected posterior state is one joint CSV row. Population replication draws
independent common and residual standard-normal matrices for the full retained
person roster, multiplies each by its saved lower-triangular correlation factor
once, then applies the saved effective-loading and residual maps plus intercepts.
The same person-level predictors are reused across all repeated observations;
each response row receives fresh Gaussian response noise. Conditional replication
uses the retained eta cells actually required by the observed rows and saved or
fixed observation scales; it does not assess population measurement structure.

The reader never factors or repairs a correlation matrix, invokes an affine
product chart, reconstructs omitted columns, regenerates source/data, fits a
model, calls a backend or executes saved model callbacks. Existing strict factor,
finite-value, positive-scale, overflow and underflow refusals are unchanged.
There is no jitter, normalization, clipping or retry to alter the declared law.

Product geometry, repeated-label unions, prior eta, measure and normalization
claims, propriety evidence, fixed subblocks, determinant/kernel/Jacobian and
initialization evidence are validated as retained records. They are not improved
or recalculated. Historical Jacobian and determinant claims retain their original
scope: one Jacobian term per unique label and the complete layer determinant,
including fixed-only subblocks. Inference stays explicitly not assessed.

## Reader evidence and authority

`bmeas_saved_iid_gaussian_product_reader_v1` is a separate exact current-reader
record in a plan's `source$reader_execution` and a result's
`metadata$reader_execution`. It specifies Gaussian-only iid scope, both levels,
canonical R kernels, joint/full-roster alignment, unchanged producer history and
experimental, unqualified inference. It is not a mutable permission switch.
Saved reader records must agree with plan/result family, level, law, model
identity and operation-specific context receipts. Malformed reached reader
records are rejected, including failed-attempt records.

Portable `ppc_plan()` is metadata planning. It performs bounded immutable counts
and schema/binding validation, then marks integrity as
`not_verified_by_this_metadata_call`. It does not hash posterior files, verify the
immutable digest, acquire states or simulate. `assess_measurement_fit()` requires
a freshly completed operation-specific context receipt, immutable digest and
protected adverse-evidence gates before active reading. Known improper or
non-normalizable evidence cannot be erased with mutable cache edits.

Archives reopen and verify authoritative archive members on every planning or
assessment call. An explicit streaming posterior budget is mandatory. Relocated
CSV members are pinned to archive digests; original paths remain historical and
are not rebound. Changed archives or CSV bytes refuse. The returned mutable cache
holds the new assessment; the input object, seals, immutable capabilities, saved
source and original history remain unchanged. Caller RNG is restored on success
and failure.

## Explicit use

Supply `ppc_plan(saved_fit, draws = 4, budget = budget, level = "population")`
or `level = "conditional"`. Execute only with an explicit seed, for example
`assess_measurement_fit(saved_fit, level = "population", draws = 4, seed = 571,
budget = budget)`. `budget` is an explicit `diagnostic_budget()`; see
[BASIC_FAMILY_PPC.md](BASIC_FAMILY_PPC.md) and [SCALABLE_PPC.md](SCALABLE_PPC.md)
for finite predictive and streaming source budgets. An external simulator is
neither required nor accepted for Gaussian-product PPC.

## Candidate evidence limits

Focused source and isolated installed-package tests use six genuine public
product declarations, terminal R-only fit doubles and coherent invented CSV
states. Coverage includes both levels, live/cold portable/relocated archive
parity, independently calculated Gaussian matrix arithmetic, selected-row keys,
repeated observations, both dependence layers, reader/binding/budget/byte-change
refusals, RNG restoration and immutable producer evidence. These fixtures are
not native posterior fits, HMC, recovery, real-study or inferential validation.
